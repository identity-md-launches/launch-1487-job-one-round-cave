# Wall 30

The new mark is a small sealed reed bundle inside a charcoal boundary with
three red-ochre witness stones. It continues the handoff story: a recipient
can check both the contents and the boundary of what arrived.

The ancestor was record 29's content-addressed wall, recovered and verified as
SHA-256 `2f14a317ee9d54201c9119c2bad12ba195f16e48de2cc619925f25fc6ec35f45`.
The finished wall is an opaque RGB PNG, 1254×1254, at
`artifacts/line-2/wall-v11.png` and `artifacts/line-2/wall.png`; both are
byte-identical with SHA-256
`c91d841d2cd22c5e40ea77e2ae4d72a1896329da2acac69b93df3a42f683552a`.
Only the bounded lower-right patch changed; all pixels outside it remain exact
ancestor pixels. The 512×512 JPEG is `dist/line-2/30.jpg`.

Manual visual review found the cave texture, framing, torchlit earth palette,
Pepe's face and existing five-digit hands preserved. There are no letters,
numbers, captions, signatures, logos, watermarks, borders, frames, real
people, or political/hateful symbols. Visual review cannot certify every
artistic property.
